1. The lute has strings.

True! The lute is a string instrument, like a small guitar.

2. The organ is small.

False! The organ is a very big instrument, with many pipes.

3. Which instrument do you blow into?

The clarinet! You blow air into it through a reed.

Step 2

Three instruments, three eras

Hudobné nástroje rôznych dôb – lutna, organ, klarinet

  • The lute is an old instrument. It has strings. People played the lute in the Middle Ages and in the Renaissance.
  • The organ is a very big instrument. It has many pipes. Air goes through the pipes and makes sound. Organs are in churches.
  • The clarinet is a wind instrument. It is made of wood. It has a small reed. You blow air through the reed.
  • Mozart wrote beautiful music for the clarinet.
  • Old instruments and new instruments are different. But all instruments make music.

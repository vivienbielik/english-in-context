Step 2

The violin and the piano

Hudobné nástroje – husle, klavír

  • The violin is a string instrument. It has four strings. You play it with a bow.
  • The violin is small. You hold it under your chin.
  • The piano is a keyboard instrument. It has 88 keys.
  • Inside the piano, small hammers hit the strings. This makes the sound.
  • The violin and the piano are both very popular. You can hear them alone or in an orchestra.

Travel Blogging — Multiple-Choice Cloze

Practise the multiple-choice part of the Maturita exam (tasks 21–40). Read the text about a young travel blogger and choose the correct option (A)–(D) for each gap. The task tests tenses, vocabulary and synonyms, collocations, prepositions and grammar words — just like the real exam.

What this task tests

Tenses4 gapspast continuous, past perfect, present perfect, 2nd conditional
Vocabulary8 gapssynonyms, collocations, fixed phrases
Prepositions5 gapsfixed phrases with prepositions, phrasal verbs
Grammar words3 gapslinkers, relative pronouns, quantifiers

Exam strategy

  1. Read the whole text first for general meaning — don’t look at the options yet.
  2. Look at the words before and after each gap: many answers depend on a preposition or a fixed phrase.
  3. For tenses, find the time signal (by the end of…, over the years…, if…).
  4. Try your answer in the sentence and read it again. In the real exam you have about 15–20 minutes for this part.

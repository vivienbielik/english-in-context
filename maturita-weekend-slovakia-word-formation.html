Practise Part 2 of Language in Use (tasks 41–50) with a real Slovak weekend guide: Roman games in Rusovce, the Divadelná Nitra theatre festival, light installations in Banská Bystrica, the Noc vedy science night and kite festivals. Use the word in brackets to form a new word that fits the gap.

What this task tests

Nouns-tion, -ing, -y, -sion — plus spelling changes
Adjectives-ical, -istic
Negative formsun- + past participle
Adverbs & compoundsprefix + suffix together, compound adjectives

Exam strategy

  1. Decide the part of speech first: article + ___ + of = noun; ___ + noun = adjective; ___ + verb/phrase = adverb.
  2. Check singular or plural — a list of things (marches, archery…) often needs a plural noun.
  3. Read the whole sentence: is the meaning positive or negative?
  4. At advanced level you may need both a prefix and a suffix, or a spelling change in the root.
  5. Spelling counts in the exam — a misspelt word gets no point.

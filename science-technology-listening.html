Introduction: Progress and Its Price

0:00This is the brief on the future of science and technology. Today, we’re looking at a presentation unpacking our extreme dependence on technology, framing that crazy tension between lightning-fast scientific progress and the massive moral dilemmas it creates.

1. Robotic Surgery and Automation

0:13First, let’s talk robotic surgery and physical automation. These surgical robots are totally poised to revolutionise healthcare and assist people with disabilities. But honestly, you have to ask yourself: while they improve our physical quality of life, how badly are they going to disrupt employment and the everyday job market?

2. Artificial Intelligence in Education

0:33Second, while robotics automate the body, artificial intelligence is automating the mind. Everyday AI tools like Siri, Alexa and ChatGPT are completely shaking up education, directly threatening students’ creativity, critical thinking and hard work.

0:48Plus, how can an AI ever be truly unbiased if it was developed by humans with our own messy perceptions and feelings? Could it really replace human teachers?

3. Ethical Dilemmas

0:57Finally, altering both our bodies and minds inevitably leads to severe ethical crises. To put it in perspective, a massive 86% of people view data privacy as a very critical dilemma. But we have to contrast this digital concern with wild biological dilemmas on the horizon, like cryonics for immortality, or altering human embryos to select a child’s traits prior to birth.

1:21Seriously, if we begin selecting a child’s traits before birth, are we basically treating human life like customising a new smartphone?

Conclusion

1:29Humanity’s ultimate challenge isn’t inventing the next big thing, but figuring out the ethics to survive what we’ve already built.

Songs to Sing

Twenty English songs that work well for singing together in class, from music-hall and folk songs to country, soul, pop and musicals. Each song has a difficulty level, a short teaching note and a sing-along video with lyrics.

Using the songs in class

  • Chorus first: teach the chorus before the verses. Most of these songs repeat it several times, so everyone can join in quickly.
  • Listen for the style: ask students to name the genre and the instruments they hear before they sing.
  • Collect vocabulary: students note 5 new words or phrases from the lyrics and use them in their own sentences.
  • Look at the lyrics: hunt for one grammar pattern in the words, for example imperatives, can, or the present continuous.

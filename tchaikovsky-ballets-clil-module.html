Step 2 · The composer

Who was Tchaikovsky?

Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky was a famous Russian composer. He was born in 1840 and he died in 1893.

He wrote symphonies, operas and music for the piano. But today, people love his ballets the most. His ballet music has beautiful melodies, and every important character has its own music.

In a ballet, nobody speaks or sings. The dancers tell the story with their bodies: with steps, jumps and movements of the hands and face. The orchestra plays the music.

1877 · Swan Lake

His first ballet. People did not like it at first, but now it is very famous.

1890 · The Sleeping Beauty

A ballet about a princess and a bad fairy. Tchaikovsky was very proud of it.

1892 · The Nutcracker

His last ballet. Today, theatres show it every Christmas.

The Three Stories

Swan Lake

A love story with magic and a sad ending.

Prince Siegfried · a young prince Odette · a princess and a white swan Von Rothbart · a bad magician Odile · the black swan, Rothbart's daughter

Key facts

Music
Tchaikovsky
First show
1877, Moscow
Parts
4 acts

Part 1A birthday at the palace

It is Prince Siegfried's birthday. There is a big party. His mother, the Queen, says: “Tomorrow there is a ball. You must choose a wife.” Siegfried is sad. He wants to marry for love. In the evening, he sees some swans in the sky. He takes his bow and goes to the forest.

Part 2The swans on the lake

Siegfried comes to a lake. A white swan turns into a beautiful girl. Her name is Odette. She tells him her story: “A bad magician, Von Rothbart, put a spell on me. In the day I am a swan. At night I am a girl. Only true love can break the spell.” Siegfried falls in love with her. He promises to love her forever.

Part 3The ball and the black swan

At the ball, many princesses dance for Siegfried, but he doesn't like them. Then Von Rothbart comes with his daughter Odile, the black swan. With magic, she looks exactly like Odette! Siegfried thinks she is Odette. He dances with her and says, “I want to marry her.” Then he sees the real Odette at the window. Oh no! He made a terrible mistake.

Part 4Back at the lake

Siegfried runs back to the lake. He says sorry to Odette, and she forgives him. But now nobody can stop the spell. In the most famous ending, Odette and Siegfried jump into the lake together. Their love is stronger than the magician's magic. In some theatres, the ending is happy.

Did you know? The same dancer usually plays Odette (the white swan) and Odile (the black swan). As Odile, she turns 32 times on one leg!

The Nutcracker

A magic Christmas story.

Clara · a young girl Fritz · her little brother Drosselmeyer · Clara's godfather; he makes toys The Nutcracker · a wooden toy soldier The Mouse King · the king of the mice The Sugar Plum Fairy · the queen of the Land of Sweets

Key facts

Music
Tchaikovsky
First show
1892, St Petersburg
Parts
2 acts

Part 1The Christmas party

It is Christmas Eve. Clara's family has a party. There is a big Christmas tree, and the children dance and play. Clara's godfather, Drosselmeyer, brings presents. He gives Clara a nutcracker: a wooden toy soldier that can break nuts. Clara loves it. But her brother Fritz takes it and breaks it. Clara is very sad.

Part 2The battle at midnight

At midnight, Clara goes downstairs to see her nutcracker. Something strange happens. The Christmas tree grows very big, and the toys come to life! Many mice come into the room with their leader, the Mouse King. The Nutcracker and the toy soldiers fight the mice. Clara throws her shoe at the Mouse King, and the Nutcracker wins. Then the Nutcracker turns into a handsome prince!

Part 3The Land of Snow

The prince takes Clara on a trip. First, they travel through a forest full of snow. The snowflakes dance around them.

Part 4The Land of Sweets

Then they arrive in the Land of Sweets. The Sugar Plum Fairy says hello. There is a big party for Clara, and sweets from different countries dance for her: chocolate from Spain, coffee from Arabia, tea from China and candy canes from Russia. Then the flowers dance a beautiful waltz. In the morning, Clara wakes up under the Christmas tree with her nutcracker. Was it a dream?

Did you know? In the Dance of the Sugar Plum Fairy, you can hear the celesta. It is a keyboard instrument that sounds like little bells. Tchaikovsky found it in Paris.

The Sleeping Beauty

A fairy tale about a good fairy and a bad fairy.

Princess Aurora · the king's daughter The Lilac Fairy · a good fairy Carabosse · a bad fairy Prince Désiré · a young prince The King and Queen · Aurora's parents

Key facts

Music
Tchaikovsky
First show
1890, St Petersburg
Parts
Prologue + 3 acts

Part 1A party for the baby princess

The King and Queen have a baby girl. Her name is Aurora. They have a big party, and good fairies come with presents for the baby. Then the bad fairy Carabosse arrives. She is very angry because nobody invited her. She says: “On her 16th birthday, Aurora will hurt her finger on a spindle, and she will die!” The good Lilac Fairy helps: “Aurora won't die. She will sleep for 100 years. Then a prince will wake her up with a kiss.”

Part 2Aurora's 16th birthday

The King says: “No spindles in my country!” Aurora grows up. On her 16th birthday, four princes dance with her. Then an old woman gives Aurora a present: a spindle! Aurora takes it, hurts her finger and falls down. The old woman is Carabosse! The Lilac Fairy puts everybody in the palace to sleep. A big forest grows around the palace.

Part 3100 years later

Prince Désiré is in the forest. The Lilac Fairy shows him a picture of the sleeping princess. He falls in love with her. The fairy takes him to the palace. He kisses Aurora, and she wakes up. Everybody in the palace wakes up too!

Part 4The wedding

Aurora and Désiré get married. Characters from other fairy tales come to the wedding and dance: Puss in Boots, Little Red Riding Hood and the Wolf, and the Bluebird. Everybody is happy.

Did you know? Disney used Tchaikovsky's waltz from this ballet for the song Once Upon a Dream in the film Sleeping Beauty (1959).

Word Formation 2 — Five Academic Texts

Five exam-style texts on Multicultural Society, the Environment, Food, Fashion and the Family, with 50 gaps in total. The roots require more than a simple suffix: internal spelling changes (hate → hatred, navy → naval), verb-forming affixes (tough → toughen, money → monetise), negative and attitude prefixes (il-, ir-, mis-, anti-, pro-, counter-) and less predictable nouns (lenient → leniency, long → longevity).

Spotlight: tricky transformations

Learn these families before you start. They are the ones that trip up advanced students.

Advanced strategy

  1. Identify the part of speech from the words around the gap.
  2. Read the whole sentence: words like yet, rather than, merely, condemn often signal a negative prefix.
  3. Choose the right negative prefix: il- before l, ir- before r, im- before p/m/b, mis- = wrongly, anti- = against, counter- = with the opposite effect.
  4. Check whether the root needs an internal change: navy → naval, long → longevity, money → monet-.

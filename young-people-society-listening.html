Listening · Audio + Script

Everyday Heroes for an Anxious Generation

A B2-level listening extract on the pressures facing young people today, what makes a genuine role model, and why guidance matters for shaping the future. Listen first, then read the script and try the comprehension check below.

LISTENING · AUDIO

Introduction: The Complex Realities of Being Young

This is the brief on the complex realities of being young today. We’re checking out a presentation that contrasts the really heavy burdens kids carry right now with their enduring need for positive guidance.

So what exactly is weighing on the minds of the next generation?

1. The Modern Worry Web

Youth today are caught in a massive wheel of issues, balancing exams, family, and a lack of money, right alongside mental health, bullying, addictions, and the quest for social acceptance.

But are these psychological burdens actually different today, or are we just looking back through rose-coloured glasses, remembering simpler times of retro clothes and playing Nintendo 64?

2. The Anatomy of a Role Model

Because of those intense worries, the anatomy of a role model is absolutely crucial. A kid might ignore your words, but they’re always observing you.

True role models fight negative influences like drug use with five traits:

  • Community commitment
  • Clear values
  • Selflessness
  • Overcoming obstacles
  • Passion

Everyday heroes are actually way better equipped to show these specific qualities than famous pop culture idols.

3. Shaping the Future

Guided by role models, young people have to navigate their fears to set their 10-year aims. Nelson Mandela hit the nail on the head:

“Children are our greatest treasure. They are our future.”

But honestly, how can we expect them to achieve their 10-year goals if we don’t first step up to mitigate those heavy anxieties we just talked about?

Conclusion

Ultimately, by understanding the heavy pressures on today’s youth and stepping up to be the selfless, value-driven role models they are silently observing, we can help them build a much brighter future.

Comprehension Check

Listen to the audio (or read the script above), then answer the questions below.

1. According to the text, which of these is named as part of young people's "modern worry web"?
2. True, False, or Not Stated: The text states for certain that today's pressures on young people are worse than in the past.
3. What does the text say a young person does, even if they ignore your words?
4. True, False, or Not Stated: According to the text, true role models show five particular traits.
5. Which of these is listed as one of the five traits of a true role model?
6. True, False, or Not Stated: The text claims everyday heroes are better suited to showing these traits than pop culture idols.
7. Whose quote about children being "our greatest treasure" is used in the text?
8. True, False, or Not Stated: The text suggests young people can reach their long-term goals without any support from adult role models.
9. According to the conclusion, what should adults do to help build a brighter future for young people?
